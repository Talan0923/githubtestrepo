Moss & Morrow began with a simple thought: caring for something green can help us care for ourselves, too.

We’re an independent neighborhood plant shop for curious beginners and longtime leaf people alike. We source resilient, responsibly grown plants and pair them with useful care advice, thoughtful pots, and the encouragement to figure it out as you go.

No perfect plant parent required. Just a bit of light, a little patience, and room to grow.
